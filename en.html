Profile

Alexis Black Aleride, Senior Researcher at Site-19, Department of Psychology. Leads the assessment teams attached to containment projects and coordinates their interview schedules and consolidated findings. Routine duties cover personnel psychological assessment, psychological evaluation and interviewing of anomalous entities, post-exposure intervention for cognitohazardous contact, and memory integrity review. Judgement rests on behavioural sequences and narrative structure rather than self-reported motive, emotional display or the impression of a single interview; where self-report conflicts with behavioural record, the record stands. Doctoral training at Harvard University in Psychology and Clinical Psychology, covering psychological tolerance to memetic hazards, narrative consistency analysis in interviews, memory integrity assessment following cognitive contamination, and psychological stability assessment systems for long-term resident personnel.

Signed assessment reports are fixed in structure and short in content — number, conclusion, necessary action, nothing further; no extended discussion, no speculative remarks. Beyond routine assessment, handles periodic review of personnel exposed to cognitohazards, watching for delayed cognitive drift. Current work centres on post-exposure personnel review. Resident at site; no external duties; rarely appears on jointly signed documents. Full record: Curriculum Vitae.
